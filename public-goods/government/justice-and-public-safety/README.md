# Justice and Public Safety

Justice and public-safety deployments can benefit from secure local AI, but they also
carry severe due-process and accountability risks.

## Good initial use cases

- policy and procedure retrieval
- report drafting assistance with human review
- records routing and document summarization
- training and tabletop exercises
- evidence-adjacent administrative support, not evidence adjudication

## Controls that matter most

- CJIS-aligned security expectations where criminal justice information is involved
- strict audit trails
- role isolation
- retention and disclosure handling
- human review on every consequential output

## Non-negotiable boundary

Do not position Citrate as a system that autonomously decides:

- guilt
- detention
- charging
- sentencing
- supervision or revocation

The safe public-sector frame here is augmentation, not automated coercion.

## Main blockers

- bias and civil-liberties concerns
- court disclosure and discoverability
- union and workforce concerns
- chain-of-custody expectations

## Candidate binaries

- CJIS-aligned report assistant
- training and policy node
- records-routing appliance
- restricted administrative document workstation

# Federal Civilian

Federal civilian use is where discipline matters most. The opportunity is large, but
the proof burden is also large.

## Good federal-civilian use cases

- grants and procurement workflow support
- records-safe internal knowledge systems
- policy and training retrieval for field offices
- research and lab collaboration on approved model artifacts
- secure document and form processing in controlled environments

## Why Citrate could fit

- local or enclave-first operation can work where agencies do not want to push every
  workflow into one shared cloud
- provenance and auditability are valuable for AI artifacts, not just financial actions
- shared framework, separate authorization boundaries is a natural federal pattern

## Controls that matter most

- NIST 800-53-aligned control evidence
- zero-trust identity and device posture
- Section 508 accessibility
- records scheduling, export, and NARA-compatible retention workflows
- PIV or hardware-backed authentication options

If the product is offered as a cloud service to federal agencies, FedRAMP becomes part
of the story. If the product is self-hosted entirely by the agency, the controls still
matter even when FedRAMP may not.

## Main blockers

- authorization packages
- continuous monitoring expectations
- machine-readable evidence for assessors
- slow procurement cycles
- understandable suspicion of any product that overclaims and under-documents

## Candidate federal-civilian binaries

- agency enclave node
- records-safe assistant
- acquisition review workspace
- federal lab model registry
- field-office restricted-network appliance

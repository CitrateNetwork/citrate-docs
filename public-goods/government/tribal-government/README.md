# Tribal Government

Tribal governments need technology that respects sovereignty, local governance,
connectivity realities, and community trust.

## Good tribal-government use cases

- sovereign records and administrative tools
- language preservation and local cultural knowledge support
- education and community-service deployments
- low-connectivity field and office operations
- local model hosting where outside cloud dependence is a nonstarter

## Why Citrate could fit

- private-network and on-prem patterns align well with sovereignty concerns
- smaller-scale deployments can still benefit from strong provenance and auditability
- air-gap-friendly or bandwidth-light operation is often more valuable than feature sprawl

## Controls that matter most

- tribal data governance
- local ownership of keys and records
- offline or intermittent-connectivity support
- simple operational playbooks for small IT teams

## Funding angle

The Tribal Cybersecurity Grant Program is a serious planning input for cyber and
restricted-network deployments, especially where resilience and local control are central.

## Main blockers

- limited staffing
- bandwidth and infrastructure disparity
- procurement bandwidth, not just procurement law
- need for trust-building before any AI or automation claims

## Candidate tribal-government binaries

- all-in-one sovereign office appliance
- offline sync node
- language and records assistant
- school-and-community shared deployment bundle

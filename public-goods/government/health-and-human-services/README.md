# Health and Human Services

Health and human-services agencies need secure automation, but they also operate under
mixed privacy, dignity, and safety constraints.

## Good initial use cases

- caseworker knowledge tools
- intake and document processing
- benefits workflow assistance
- public-health planning and playbooks
- multilingual assistance for administrative interactions

## Why Citrate could fit

- local or restricted-network inference can reduce unnecessary exposure of sensitive data
- strong auditability helps where case handling must be reviewable
- agency-owned deployment can be easier to justify than broad external AI dependence

## Controls that matter most

- HIPAA where ePHI is involved
- minimum-necessary access patterns
- accessibility and language access
- retention, deletion, and records export
- strong human oversight for vulnerable-population workflows

## Main blockers

- mixed data regimes across agencies
- complex consent and authority chains
- high consequence of bad summaries or missed context
- need for clinician, social-worker, and legal review in many flows

## Candidate binaries

- caseworker assistant
- clinic knowledge node
- benefits document processor
- public-health restricted-network planner

# Defense and National Security

This sector needs the most caution.

The opportunity is real:

- enclave-hosted inference
- maintenance and logistics assistance
- training environments
- coalition-safe sharing of approved model artifacts

But the public claims must stay conservative.

## Good initial use cases

- maintenance and technical manual retrieval
- training simulators and classroom support
- logistics planning assistants
- base-operations workflow support
- provenance-checked model exchange for approved unclassified environments

## Controls that matter most

- NIST 800-171 and related defense-contractor expectations
- CMMC where contractors are in scope
- DoD zero-trust identity and device posture
- removable-media and offline-patching procedures
- export-control and country-risk review before any allied exchange

## What we should not claim

- not classified-network ready by default
- not IL5 or IL6 by implication
- not export-clear for any model package
- not suitable for autonomous operational or lethal decision-making

## Main blockers

- export controls
- cross-domain approvals
- hardware-chain trust
- enclave packaging and update discipline
- much higher assurance expectations around identity, logging, and configuration control

## Candidate defense-adjacent binaries

- enclave inference appliance
- secure training workstation
- maintenance knowledge node
- coalition model exchange node for approved unclassified artifacts

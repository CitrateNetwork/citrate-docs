# Learning Center District Onboarding

This is the target public onboarding motion for a district deployment.

The promise is simple:

- no forced cloud dependency for core institutional control
- no hand-wavy privacy story
- no "call us back after six months of integration" sales motion

## Target deployment outcome

A district signs, preflights the privacy and IT package, installs the app, provisions
users, and launches a pilot cohort without rewriting its identity or infrastructure stack.

## Phase 1: Governance and procurement

District outcomes:

- identify executive sponsor
- identify IT owner
- identify privacy or legal reviewer
- identify pilot school and staff cohort

Citrate deliverables:

- product overview
- privacy and security summary
- district onboarding checklist
- draft contract and DPA posture

## Phase 2: Privacy and legal

District outcomes:

- approve the data-sharing posture
- confirm parent and student rights workflow
- approve retention and deletion language
- confirm incident notification expectations

Citrate deliverables:

- school-official operating model
- contract language aligned to educational purpose and redisclosure limits
- parent and eligible-student access design
- state-specific addenda where required

## Phase 3: IT preflight

District outcomes:

- choose deployment mode: connected, restricted, or private-network
- confirm packaging path: MDM, software center, package repo, or image
- confirm firewall, proxy, and certificate requirements
- confirm roster source and import format

Citrate deliverables:

- installer packages
- health check commands
- network whitelist
- roster import format guide
- device registration and recovery procedure

## Phase 4: Pilot launch

District outcomes:

- provision IT, admin, teacher, and student pilot accounts
- run one classroom, one budget, one roster, and one parent/guardian access drill
- validate support contacts and rollback path

Citrate deliverables:

- pilot runbook
- role-based quick starts
- audit and event review guidance
- issue triage matrix

## Phase 5: District expansion

District outcomes:

- expand by school or department
- validate training and help-desk readiness
- confirm reporting and retention procedures

Citrate deliverables:

- revised deployment package based on pilot findings
- broader training material
- upgrade and maintenance cadence
- roadmap review for parent portal, SDK, and optional on-prem AI

## Current reality

This onboarding motion is the intended public deployment shape.

As of 2026-04-09:

- the compliance package exists in internal form
- the contract stack and Learning Center flows exist
- the packaging, parent portal, and full P-1 proof harness are still the near-term finish work

That distinction matters. The onboarding story is real, but some of its deployment
artifacts are still being converted from plan to finished deliverable.

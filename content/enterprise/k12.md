# K-12 & Education — Privacy and Compliance

> How the Citrate Learning Center is designed for schools: districts keep control of
> student data, and contracts plus technical controls limit secondary use. Public summary;
> the deeper district-onboarding / KYC track is commercial.

> **Disclaimer.** This is a design-and-operations summary for district counsel, IT, and
> procurement teams — not legal advice and not a claim of certification.

## Overview

The Learning Center is the school-facing product track: a district-operable, role-gated,
privacy-aware interface for classroom operations. The core privacy promise:

- **No student data on the network.** Student records stay under school / LEA control on
  district-selected infrastructure (on-prem, restricted-network, or a private Citrate
  deployment). Nothing about a student is published to a public chain or a vendor-operated
  service.
- **Role-gated, least-privilege by default.** Contract-level role checks in the education stack;
  auditable on-chain events for key institutional actions (not for student PII).
- **Educational purpose only.** Data use is tied to the school's delegated educational purpose —
  **no targeted advertising, no unrelated profiling, no hidden secondary monetization.**
- **AI features are optional** and must not weaken the privacy baseline.

## Compliance mapping (public posture)

| Law | Posture | Honest caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **FERPA** | District remains the decision-maker; vendor acts only within the school's delegated educational purpose under the familiar "school official" path with a written agreement. Parent / eligible-student inspect-and-review access is part of the baseline, not an optional add-on. | An attorney-reviewed DPA template is **not yet complete**; the guardian portal is planned and security-scoped but not yet the finished public baseline. |
| **COPPA** | In the educational context and for the school's benefit, schools may act as the parent's agent for consent — which only holds because the product posture forbids unrelated commercial use of student information. | Depends on schools operating within that educational-context boundary. |
| **CIPA** | App-level content controls can help, but the honest rule is that they **do not replace** a district's internet-safety policy, filtering, monitoring, and records obligations. We do not imply CIPA compliance from "AI safety" alone. | Districts relying on E-Rate retain real operational obligations. |
| **State student-privacy contracts (e.g., CA AB 1584)** | Pupil records remain LEA property and under LEA control; contracts describe review, correction, breach notice, retention, deletion; targeted advertising against pupil records is prohibited. We treat this as the right contract posture even outside California. | State-specific filings are **not yet complete** for every state. |

## Honest gaps still being closed

- Attorney-reviewed DPA template not yet complete.
- State-specific filings incomplete for some states.
- Guardian portal planned/scoped, not yet finished as the public baseline.
- End-to-end integration and live smoke gates under construction.
- Federal / defense-grade packages would require controls beyond school compliance.

## Going deeper (commercial track)

District onboarding, the data-processing agreement, and identity/KYC for staff and guardians are
handled on the **commercial** track and gated behind contract + KYC (`commercial.kyc`). This public
page covers posture and design intent; deployment-specific terms are not authored here. See
[DPA / SLA / subprocessors](/enterprise/dpa) (gated) and [Procurement](/enterprise/procurement).
